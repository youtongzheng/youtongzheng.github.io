Youtong Zheng is an Assistant Professor of atmospheric science at the University of Houston, working on clouds, convection, and precipitation and their roles in weather and climate through modeling, theory, observations, and machine learning.

## Education

- **Ph.D., Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences**, University of Maryland, College Park, 2018. Advisers: Zhanqing Li and Daniel Rosenfeld.
- **B.S., Atmospheric Science**, Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology (formerly Nanjing Institute of Meteorology), China, 2012.

## Positions

- **2023–present:** Assistant Professor, Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences, University of Houston.
- **2023–2024:** Visiting Research Collaborator, Program in Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, Princeton University.
- **2021–2023:** Associate Research Scholar, Program in Atmospheric and Oceanic Sciences, Princeton University, and NOAA Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory.
- **2018–2021:** Postdoctoral Associate (2018–2020) and Assistant Research Scientist (2020–2021), Earth System Science Interdisciplinary Center, University of Maryland.
- **2013–2014:** Visiting student, Institute of Earth Sciences, Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Israel.

## Awards and honors

- **2025** Presidential Circle Award, University of Houston.
- **2023** Early Career Award, U.S. Department of Energy, Office of Science (one of the top two university awardees in the Biological and Environmental Research program).
- **2022** Presidential Frontier Faculty Professorship, University of Houston.
- **2021** Finalist, Lawrence Fellowship, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory.
- **2019** Yuxiang Young Scholar Award, Chinese-American Oceanic and Atmospheric Association (top 5 Chinese earth scientists within five years of the Ph.D.).
- **2017** Ann G. Wylie Dissertation Fellowship, University of Maryland.
- **2017** Chinese Government Award for Outstanding Students Abroad, China Scholarship Council (top ~500 worldwide from more than 500,000 applicants).
- **2016** Outstanding Graduate Assistant Award (top 2%), University of Maryland.
- **2016** Ann Wylie Green Fund Scholarship Award, ESSIC/University of Maryland.
- Travel awards: NASA Langley (ACAM 2017), Nanjing University Young Scientist Forum (2017), Jacob Goldhaber Travel Award (2016), AGU Fall Meeting Travel Grant (2015).
