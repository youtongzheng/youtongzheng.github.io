Courses taught at the University of Houston, Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences.

GEOL 4349/6397: Cloud Physics

How clouds form, how they evolve over time, and how they influence weather and climate.

GEOL 4352/6397: Synoptic Meteorology

The atmospheric processes behind winds, temperature change, and precipitation; the development and decay of mid-latitude storms and fronts, and their hazards.

GEOL 4333/6364: Mesoscale Meteorology

The physics and practical consequences of mesoscale weather: systems smaller than a cyclone but larger than a turbulent eddy, such as thunderstorms, squall lines, and sea breezes.

GEOL 1347: Introduction to Meteorology

High- and low-pressure systems, air masses, clouds, the jet stream, precipitation, air–sea interactions, local and global circulation, and weather analysis.
