Students understand the atmosphere best when they reason their way to it. Each lecture is built as a chain of questions, and at every turning point the question goes to the class. In large classes, live polls and short forecasting exercises with real weather maps keep everyone thinking.

Courses at the University of Houston

GEOL 1347: Introduction to Meteorology

High- and low-pressure systems, air masses, clouds, the jet stream, precipitation, air–sea interactions, local and global circulation, and weather analysis. A general-education course open to all majors.

“I was very fascinated with meteorology at a young age. I lived on the Gulf Coast and hurricanes blew through there. That is the class I failed in college: meteorology.”
— Jim Parsons, University of Houston alumnus

Taught: Fall 2023, Spring 2025, Fall 2026

GEOL 4349/6397: Cloud Physics new course

How clouds form, how droplets grow into rain, and how clouds shape weather and climate.

Taught: Spring 2024, Spring 2025

GEOL 4352/6397: Synoptic Meteorology (with lab) new course

The atmospheric processes behind winds, temperature change, and precipitation; the development and decay of mid-latitude storms and fronts, and their hazards. A core course of UH's new B.S. in Atmospheric Science.

Taught: Fall 2024, Fall 2025, Fall 2026

GEOL 6364/4333: Mesoscale Meteorology and Forecasting new course

Weather systems smaller than a cyclone but larger than a turbulent eddy, where extreme weather happens: thunderstorms, squall lines, and sea breezes, and how to forecast them.

Taught: Spring 2026

Mentoring

Students in the group own a real research question, with more guidance at the start and more independence as they grow. They are also trained for the field they will enter: in our weekly AI Agent Day, everyone learns to use AI agents in research and to check every result against evidence the AI did not make.

Recent student honors include a DOE Office of Science Graduate Student Research (SCGSR) fellowship at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, an AMS Outstanding Student Presentation Award, a national-laboratory summer internship, and several university scholarships and travel awards. Meet the group.
