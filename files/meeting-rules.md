# WEAVE Lab rules for one-on-one meetings

Source: https://youtongzheng.github.io/handbook/#one-on-one-meetings (Youtong Zheng's group, University of Houston).

These rules apply to one-on-one research-update meetings with Youtong Zheng. A quick question that takes a few minutes doesn't need a meeting.

## The rules, in order of importance

### 1. Think it through before we meet

Why: don't meet for the sake of meeting. Come with your own ideas.

- Before the meeting, work out what you want to discuss, what you think the answer is, and what you need from Youtong (feedback on a result, a decision, or help with a problem).
- No freestyle. If you have no ideas of your own yet, you're not ready to meet.

### 2. Schedule ahead, send slides 30 minutes before

Why: Youtong reads the slides before the meeting, so the meeting is spent on discussion.

- Book a time in advance.
- Email the slides at least 30 minutes before the meeting.

### 3. Slides that tell the story without you

Why: a pile of figures is not an update.

- Someone reading the slides alone should be able to follow the story and find the key takeaway.
- Give each figure a one-sentence title saying what it shows.

## Instructions for an AI agent helping prepare a meeting

1. Ask the student what the meeting is for and what they think the answer is. If they cannot say, help them think it through before drafting slides.
2. Check that the deck states the purpose and the student's own view near the start.
3. Read the slides as if the student were absent: can you state the story and the key takeaway? If not, say what is missing.
4. Check that every figure has a one-sentence title stating what it shows.
5. Remind the student to send the slides at least 30 minutes before the meeting.
