# Agent instructions for this site

- The source of truth for career facts is the `Portfolio/` folder in Y. Zheng's Obsidian vault (Second Brain). Update the site from it; never invent facts.
- Public content only: no pending or declined proposals, manuscripts under review, review or committee details, student grades, or application status.
- New paper: add an entry at the top of `_data/publications.yml` with the next `P##` ID used in `Portfolio/Publications.md`; mark supervised students/postdocs with `<u>…</u>` and the corresponding author with `*`.
- New news item: add it to the top of `_pages/news.md`; keep the latest ~7 items in the "Recent news" list on `_pages/about.md`.
- Group changes: edit `_pages/group.md` (move leavers to Alumni with their next position).
- Do not use pronouns for people unless they are stated; write in English.
- Push to `master` only with Y. Zheng's approval; GitHub Pages rebuilds in about a minute.
