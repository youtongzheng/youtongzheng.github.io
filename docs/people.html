Youtong Zheng

Principal Investigator

Youtong Zheng is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences at the University of Houston (since 2023). Before that, Youtong was an Associate Research Scholar at Princeton University and NOAA's Geophysical Fluid Dynamics Laboratory (2021–2023), and a postdoc and Assistant Research Scientist at the University of Maryland (2018–2021). Youtong received a Ph.D. from the University of Maryland in 2018, advised by Zhanqing Li, and a B.S. from Nanjing University of Information Science and Technology. Honors include a DOE Early Career Award (2023) and the University of Houston Presidential Circle Award (2025). Youtong is an Associate Editor of the Journal of Climate.

How we work

  • Weekly group meeting on Friday mornings. Slides are shared before the meeting, with variables and methods defined.
  • AI Agent Day on Friday afternoons: we try AI agents on our own research, present what worked and what did not, and turn repeated analyses into checked, trusted tools.
  • Writing: everyone reads Joshua Schimel's Writing Science, and papers are written as stories with one clear point.
  • Training: the group supports travel to conferences, hackathons, and summers at national laboratories.
