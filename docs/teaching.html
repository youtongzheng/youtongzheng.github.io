Teaching

Youtong teaches meteorology as a chain of concept, tool, and decision: understand the physics, learn the tool that measures or predicts it, then use both to make a forecast or a decision. Lectures are built around questions. Students answer live polls during class (Top Hat, Mentimeter), work through short forecasting exercises with real weather maps, and get practice quizzes and short videos to review.

Since 2024 Youtong has developed three new courses for UH's B.S. in Atmospheric Science: Cloud Physics, Synoptic Meteorology (with lab), and Mesoscale Meteorology and Forecasting. Synoptic Meteorology is a core course of the new major and is designed to meet the federal GS-1340 meteorologist requirement used by the National Weather Service and the American Meteorological Society's guidelines for a bachelor's degree in atmospheric science.
