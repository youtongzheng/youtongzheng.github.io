WEAVE Lab · Weaving Empirical and Virtual Evidence

Youtong Zheng 郑又通

Assistant Professor, Department of Earth and Atmospheric Sciences, University of Houston

We study clouds, convection, and precipitation, and their roles in weather and climate. The WEAVE Lab combines observations, high-resolution simulations, theory, and machine learning, because the atmosphere is understood best when different kinds of evidence are woven together. Much of our current work is on the thunderstorms of the Texas Gulf Coast and on whether the models we use to predict them get the right answer for the right physical reason.
